VOIDBREAKERS - THE LAST FRONTIER

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click Voidbreakers.html. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
3. Click "Launch survival", then click inside the game to capture the mouse.

CONTROLS
  W A S D ........ move
  Mouse .......... aim
  Left click ..... main weapon (hold to charge a railgun)
  Right click .... second weapon
  Space .......... dash
  Q .............. ship ability
  E .............. dock at the station (sell ore, buy upgrades)
  T .............. evolution tree (unlock and evolve ships)
  R .............. drones: escort or attack
  Tab / Esc ...... pause

TIPS
- Shoot asteroids for ore, fly over it to collect, dock to sell it.
- Credits and crystal buy new ships in the evolution tree.
- Every 5th wave brings a boss: destroy its shield generators first.
- Your progress saves in your browser. Keep the file in the same folder so you don't lose it.

Needs a computer with a mouse and keyboard. Works offline.
